Joshua Mcshannon Joins the PSF as Infrastructure Engineer

<p>
  The Python Software Foundation (PSF) is excited to announce our newest staff
  member and Infrastructure Engineer,
  <a href="https://github.com/jmcshannon">Joshua Mcshannon</a>, reporting to
  Director of Engineering Jacob Coffee. Joshua brings over 16 years of technical
  expertise from various backgrounds including bootstrapped startups and big
  tech. Most recently he built an AI-integrated learning platform for
  cybersecurity students and taught secure web application development along
  with DevOps/platform engineering.
</p>

<p>
  Joshua will focus on reducing operational complexity and toil of the PSF
  infrastructure, optimizing to better serve the community at large and internal
  PSF platforms that CPython core developers and staff members rely on daily;
  this includes services like <a href="https://python.org">python.org</a>, PyPI,
  and infrastructure behind
  <a href="https://pycon.blogspot.com/2026/09/pycon-us-2026-recap-and-recordings.html">PyCon US</a>.
  With Joshua on the team, the PSF will gain much needed redundancy for on-call
  rotations as well as capacity for new infrastructure projects.
</p>

<p><b><i>“We put people on the moon with a Ti-82, I’m sure we can figure something out.”&nbsp;</i></b></p>

<p>
  Joshua has already shown great initiative in the work that the PSF has, diving
  deep into our Kubernetes infrastructure and preparing improvements to keep
  costs low. Away from the keyboard you’ll find him at a local show, on a nature
  trail, or at a flea market with his family. Say hello when you see Joshua in
  the repos, issue trackers, mailing lists, and on
  <a href="https://discuss.python.org">discuss.python.org</a>!
</p>
