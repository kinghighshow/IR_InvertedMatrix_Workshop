Announcing the PSF Strategic Plan 2026

<p style="text-align: left;">In May, the Python Software Foundation (PSF)&nbsp;<a href="https://pyfound.blogspot.com/2026/05/strategic-planning-at-psf.html">shared the high-level goals</a> of  our Strategic Plan. In June, we&nbsp;<a href="https://pyfound.blogspot.com/2026/06/psf-strategic-plan-2026-draft-open-for.html">published the full draft</a> and opened it for community feedback. Today we are sharing the outcome: at its July 8 meeting, the PSF Board adopted the <a href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1NxkgWzR7j3K8W8wp0j41VyjNtrw-sOlY7loXTd-J3iI/edit" target="_blank">PSF Strategic Plan 2026</a>, covering 2026 to 2031.</p><p style="text-align: left;">Our new <a href="https://www.python.org/psf/strategy/">PSF Strategy page</a> is the permanent home for the plan, with information about how the board sets priorities and how you can share feedback. We’ll publish annual review findings and future updates there so the community can follow our progress and any changes to the plan.</p><p></p><h2 style="text-align: left;">What the feedback changed</h2><p style="text-align: left;">Feedback from PSF Staff and the Python community shaped real changes to the draft we published in June.</p><p style="text-align: left;">Staff feedback added a Security Baseline objective for all PSF projects and services and a Vulnerability Management objective in response to the rapid growth in security reports, and it sharpened wording across the organizational goals. Community feedback shaped the plan, too: translation and localization are now part of the accessibility work, companies that want to fund specific work get clearer pathways, and the ideas under financial sustainability now include growing the value of PSF membership.</p><p style="text-align: left;">The plan's <b>"How This Plan Was Shaped"</b> section documents the process, and we are grateful to everyone who took the time to share their perspectives.</p><h2 style="text-align: left;">The annual review</h2><p style="text-align: left;">A five-year plan is only useful if it stays current. Alongside adoption, the board established an annual review of the strategic plan. Each year, the board will:</p><ul style="text-align: left;"><li><p>Assess progress against the goals</p></li><li><p>Evaluate whether priorities need to shift</p></li><li><p>Incorporate feedback from PSF staff and the community</p></li><li><p>Publish a summary of findings and any changes made</p></li></ul><p style="text-align: left;">The plan is designed as a living document, and we will be sharing details on the first review cycle.</p><h2 style="text-align: left;">What happens next</h2><p style="text-align: left;">Implementation of the Strategic Plan is the job of PSF staff, and it has already started. The&nbsp;<a href="https://pyfound.blogspot.com/2026/07/announcing-2026-psf-grants-program.html">2026 Grants Program Funding Round</a> announced in July is the first concrete step under the plan's grants reform direction, and awards will be announced later this month.</p><p style="text-align: left;">We also know the community is waiting for updated financial information. The PSF has engaged an external accounting firm to support this work, and we will share an update as soon as the numbers are&nbsp;ready for publication.</p><p style="text-align: left;">The plan now informs how the PSF allocates its budget and staff time, and there are several ways to stay involved. We welcome community feedback at strategy@python.org year-round, and the monthly&nbsp;<a href="https://pyfound.blogspot.com/2025/10/a-new-psf-board-another-year-of-psf.html">PSF Board Office Hours</a> on the PSF Discord are a good place to connect with the board and to ask questions about the plan. Input arriving now feeds the first annual review.</p><p style="text-align: left;">Thank you to everyone who read the draft, contributed feedback, joined the office hours, and talked with us at PyCon US. The PSF’s Strategic Plan is better for it.</p><p style="text-align: left;"><i>Jannis Leidel, PSF Board Chair, on behalf of the PSF Board of Directors</i></p>
