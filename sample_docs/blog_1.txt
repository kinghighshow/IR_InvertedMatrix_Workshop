Reopening the PSF Grants Program with Twice-Yearly Funding Rounds

<p><b>The Python Software Foundation (PSF) is excited to announce that the PSF Grants Program is reopening with a restructured schedule of two funding rounds per year.</b> After the <a href="https://pyfound.blogspot.com/2025/08/the-psf-has-paused-our-grants-program.html" target="_blank">pause in 2025</a>, and the <a href="https://pyfound.blogspot.com/2026/07/announcing-2026-psf-grants-program.html" target="_blank">special funding round held in 2026</a>, the PSF is reopening the PSF Grants Program on an ongoing but restructured basis, with the budget reviewed annually. This approach aims to balance the sustainability of the program with our commitment to support the Python community through grants.&nbsp;<br /><br />The PSF is also happy to announce the upcoming funding round with a budget capped at $125,000 USD with a similar scope as the special funding round. <b>We will be accepting applications from December 14 - January 4, for Conferences and Workshops that are scheduled between May 1, 2027, and September 30, 2027.</b> Details on the restructured program are below. More information about the upcoming round will be published next week.&nbsp;</p><p></p><h3 style="text-align: left;">Moving forward with twice-yearly funding rounds</h3><p>The <a href="https://pyfound.blogspot.com/2026/07/announcing-2026-psf-grants-program.html" target="_blank">2026 PSF Grants Program Funding Round</a> was a success! The PSF is proud to have awarded 44 grants totaling approximately $60,000. The round also gave the Grants Work Group (GWG) and PSF staff the opportunity to explore the round-based process, in contrast to our old process of rolling applications, review, and awards. We received many questions and much feedback during the Grants Program Office Hour sessions and via email before and during the round. The most frequent question we received was “when will the next round of funding happen?”<br /><br />While all the PSF could share at the time was that we hoped for the program to run on an ongoing basis, what we felt this question pointed to was the community’s call for reliability from the PSF Grants Program. The PSF wants to meet this call, both for the community’s sake and because reliability is one of <a href="https://www.python.org/psf/grants/#guiding-principles" target="_blank">the Program’s Guiding Principles</a>. It also supports our <a href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1NxkgWzR7j3K8W8wp0j41VyjNtrw-sOlY7loXTd-J3iI/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.kq4bn6q8o0kt" target="_blank">Strategic Plan goal</a> of fostering a thriving, connected global Python community. Based on that, we intend to run two funding rounds per year going forward, with the budget to be set in advance each year. That way, we can reliably offer grants to the community, and each round’s budget will determine how many grants we can award and at what size. We look forward to receiving community feedback so we can continue to improve the restructured PSF Grants Program as we move forward.&nbsp;</p><p></p><h3 style="text-align: left;">Round timing</h3><p>In order to get on a schedule aligned with PSF staff capacity around other projects throughout the year, (such as PyCon US, elections, and our end-of-year fundraiser), the upcoming second funding round will cover a five-month eligibility period of May 2027 through September 2027, meaning events taking place within that window can apply. After that, each round will cover six months, October to March and April to September. Rounds 3 and 4 below set the schedule we expect to follow going forward, repeating each year. The table shows the previous round, the upcoming round, and our expectations for the rounds after that.
